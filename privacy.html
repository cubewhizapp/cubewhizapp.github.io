Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

CubeWhiz is published by Muhammad Kashif Ali, who is responsible for this policy.

In short: CubeWhiz does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.

Camera and photos

CubeWhiz uses your camera only while you are scanning a cube. Each camera frame is read on your device to measure the colours in the nine sticker spots and is then discarded. No pictures or video are saved or sent anywhere. If you choose a photo from your library, CubeWhiz reads only the photo you pick, on your device, and does not keep a copy.

Information stored on your device

Your settings, progress (solves, lessons and challenges), timer results and any unfinished solve are saved on your device only. They are not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes them. You can also clear them in Settings → Reset progress and times.

Purchases

CubeWhiz Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether Premium has been bought.

Children

CubeWhiz has a kids mode and is suitable for children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children. In kids mode, purchases, settings and links to websites are behind a parental gate.

Third parties

CubeWhiz contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: cubewhiz.help@outlook.com
